# R267–R268 — Prepared comparisons, unresolved acquisition

Research continues under the user's instruction until interruption. The previous published fixed baseline is R266. These stages add preparation and source-derived inference limits; they do not enable VCN or establish a new physical observation.

## Confirmed

- R267: standard DRM information-query preflight compiles with warnings treated as errors; it was not executed. Passive cached device/module metadata matches the earlier baseline identity, not its runtime state.
- R267: saved-counter arithmetic passes 1,692 independent small-width increment-enumeration cases, eight named controls and four invalid-input controls. These are CPU checks over synthetic data, not a replay of Thomas's measurements.
- R268: pinned ordinary driver source dispatches three VCN interrupt sources to fence processing and uses two LMI-status waits in the stop path. These are not first-instruction observation contracts.

## Strong evidence

The earlier attributed Thomas GPCNT/RBC positive results continue to demote whole-domain clock absence. The Shalasere final archive remains a distinct board-specific baseline. No new evidence here merges their hardware states or changes the cause assessment.

## Still unknown

Original GPCNT measurement code and count/timestamp pairs, the validated selector mapping, trace visibility, effective VCPU reset/leaf clock, first-fetch address/completion and execution remain unresolved. New GPCNT/SMN acquisition is not ready. Prepared components are an ordinary API preflight, saved-record templates, arithmetic analysis and A/B interpretation.

## Contradicted / deprioritized hypotheses

Do not infer first fetch from LMI clean bits, a generic interrupt or a register name. Do not infer no fetch from an unvalidated negative observer. Do not use requested frequency to select a counter wrap count. A positive request excludes inability to emit that request only within its attributable trial/time/reset epoch.

Direct harvesting-clear variants remain outside the main research line. No new MMIO/SMN/SMU/PSP requests, firmware loading, video jobs or boot changes occurred in these stages.

## Best discriminating next experiments

First reuse original saved records: replay the two GPCNT rates with independent timing, compare same-trial mirror values across boards, and evaluate the planned stock/modified BIOS control without treating BIOS difference as uniquely a warm-reset difference. A first-fetch observer additionally needs initiator/address/completion attribution and its own positive control.
